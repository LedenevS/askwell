# Billing FAQ

**Which payment methods do you accept?** Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Annual Scale plans can also be paid by bank transfer (invoice terms are net 30).

**Can I get an invoice?** Yes — invoices are generated automatically for every payment and are available under Settings → Billing → Invoices. You can add your company name, address and VAT number so they appear on every invoice.

**Do you charge VAT?** For customers in the EU without a valid VAT number we charge VAT at the local rate. Customers who provide a valid VAT number are billed under the reverse-charge mechanism.

**What happens if my payment fails?** We retry the card on days 3, 5 and 7. You get an email each time. If all retries fail, the workspace is downgraded to Free — nothing is deleted, and upgrading again restores your plan immediately.

**How do I cancel?** Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. You keep your paid features until the end of the billing period, then move to Free. There are no cancellation fees.

**Do you offer refunds?** Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within the first 30 days. Email billing@nimbus.example.

**Can I change the billing email?** Yes, under Settings → Billing → Billing contact. Receipts go to that address in addition to the owner.

**Where do I see how many events I've used?** Settings → Billing shows current-month usage and a projection for the end of the month. You'll also receive an email at 80% and 100% of your allowance.
