# Teams, roles and permissions

## Inviting teammates
Owners and admins can invite people from Settings → Members → Invite. Enter one or more email addresses and choose a role. Invitations expire after 7 days and can be resent. There is no limit on the number of seats on any plan — seats are free.

People who sign up with a verified email at your company's domain can also request access; an owner or admin approves or declines the request from the same page.

## Roles
- **Owner** — full access, including billing, deleting the workspace and transferring ownership. There must always be at least one owner.
- **Admin** — everything except billing and deleting the workspace. Admins manage members, projects and data sources.
- **Editor** — can create and edit dashboards, charts and alerts in the projects they have access to.
- **Viewer** — read-only access to dashboards. Viewers can subscribe to digests and use public share links.

Project-level access: on Growth and Scale you can restrict a member to specific projects. On Free, all members see all projects.

## Transferring ownership
The current owner can transfer ownership from Settings → Members by choosing another member and selecting “Make owner”. The previous owner becomes an admin.

## Removing someone
Admins and owners can remove a member at any time. Their dashboards and alerts stay in the workspace and are reassigned to the person who removed them.

## Guests and public links
Any dashboard can be shared with a public, read-only link (Settings → Sharing on the dashboard). Public links can be password-protected and revoked at any time. Viewers of public links do not count as members.
