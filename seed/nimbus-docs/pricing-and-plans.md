# Nimbus pricing and plans

Nimbus has three plans. All prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT where applicable.

## Free
- $0 forever
- Up to 100,000 tracked events per month
- 1 project, unlimited seats
- 3 dashboards, 3 alerts
- 30 days of data retention
- Community support

## Growth — $99 per month
- Up to 2 million tracked events per month
- Unlimited projects, dashboards and alerts
- 12 months of data retention
- Weekly digests by email and Slack
- Email support with a one-business-day response target
- Billed monthly, or $990 per year (two months free)

## Scale — $499 per month
- Up to 20 million tracked events per month
- Everything in Growth
- 3 years of data retention
- SSO with SAML and SCIM user provisioning
- Audit log and data export to your warehouse (BigQuery, Snowflake, Redshift)
- Priority support with a four-hour response target and a shared Slack channel
- Billed monthly, or $4,990 per year

## Overages
If you exceed your monthly event allowance, tracking continues — we never drop your data. Overage is billed at $0.50 per 10,000 events on Growth and $0.30 per 10,000 events on Scale. Free workspaces that exceed 100,000 events are paused until the next month or until they upgrade.

## Trials
Every new workspace gets a 14-day trial of Growth with no credit card required. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to Free automatically. Nothing is deleted.

## Discounts
- Annual billing saves roughly 17% (two months free).
- Startups under two years old with less than $2M in funding get 50% off Growth for the first year — apply from Settings → Billing.
- Registered non-profits and educational institutions get 50% off any plan.

## Changing plans
You can upgrade at any time from Settings → Billing; the change takes effect immediately and the price is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Cancelling moves you to Free — your data is kept for the retention period of the Free plan (30 days).
